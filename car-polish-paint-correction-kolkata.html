Polish and paint correction, explained

Over time, washing, drying and everyday dust leave fine circular marks in a car's clear coat. These are called swirl marks, and they show up as a dull finish in the sun. Paint correction uses machine buffing and polishing to level the surface and bring back clarity and gloss.

Results depend on the condition of your paint. Machine correction can reduce swirl marks and light to moderate scratches. Very deep scratches that go through the clear coat can often be reduced but not fully erased. We will always tell you what to expect.
