Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

Where is MyPod Detailing Studio?

We are at Action Area I, Newtown, Kolkata, West Bengal. You can also book a doorstep service anywhere inside our service area in New Town.

Do you come to my home or office?

Yes. Doorstep service covers home, office and society parking in Action Area I, Action Area II and CBD, and Action Area III in New Town. We serve PIN codes 700156, 700157, 700160, 700161 and 700135. Enter your PIN in the booking form and it confirms instantly. If your PIN is not covered, you can choose the studio instead.

Which areas and housing complexes do you serve?

We serve Action Area I (including Axis Mall, DLF 1, Nazrul Tirtha, Alaktika and Animikha), Action Area II and CBD (including Biswa Bangla Gate, Eco Park, City Centre 2, Elita Garden Vista, Shrachi Rosedale and Sunrise Symphony), and Action Area III (including Unitech Gate 1, Tata Medical Center, Shapoorji Sukhobrishti, Uniworld City, Sanjeeva Orchards and Hiland Woods).

How do I book a wash?

Choose car or bike, your vehicle size and a package. Then add your location, pick a live time slot, enter your details and confirm. The whole thing takes about a minute.

How will I know my booking is confirmed?

You get a booking ID and a confirmation email instantly. We also email reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before your slot.

How can I pay?

Pay with UPI, using any UPI app or the QR code, or choose to pay cash after the service is done.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Enter your booking ID and the mobile number you booked with on our website to check or cancel your booking. Cancelling frees the slot for someone else.

How much does a car wash cost?

Car packages start at ₹550 for a Basic Wash on a hatchback. Prices depend on your vehicle size and the package. See the full price table on our car wash page.

How much does a bike wash cost?

Bike packages start at ₹250 for Go Premium on a naked bike. See all bike packages and prices on our bike wash page.

Do you clean pet hair?

Our car packages include interior cleaning, and extra charges may apply for pet hair removal.

Do you offer polishing and paint correction?

Yes. Exterior Gold and Ultimate Revival Detail for cars, and Ultimate Pods for bikes, include paint correction. Results depend on the condition of your paint.

Can I use a flyer or promo code?

Yes. If you have a MyPod flyer code, enter it during booking to apply your offer. Codes are optional.
