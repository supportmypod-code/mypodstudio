Doorstep car and bike wash in Newtown, Kolkata

Skip the trip. We come to your home, office or society parking, so your ride is cared for while you get on with your day.

How doorstep service works

  1. Choose your package. Pick your car or bike and the care you want. The price shows straight away.
  2. Share your location and PIN code. Use the current-location button in the booking form and we fill in your address. Then add your house or flat number, a landmark and your PIN code.
  3. Pick a live slot. Choose a day and time from the open slots.
  4. Confirm. You get a booking ID and a confirmation email instantly. We also email reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before your slot.
  5. We arrive. Our crew heads to you about 30 minutes before your slot starts.

Where we come

Doorstep service covers Action Area I, Action Area II and CBD, and Action Area III in New Town (Newtown), Kolkata. That includes home, office and society parking in housing complexes such as Shapoorji Sukhobrishti, Uniworld City, Shrachi Rosedale and Elita Garden Vista. See the full list of areas we serve.

We serve PIN codes 700156, 700157, 700160, 700161 and 700135. Enter your PIN in the booking form and it confirms instantly whether we can come to you. If your PIN is not on the list, you can choose our studio instead.

What you can book at your door

Car and bike packages are available for doorstep booking. See the full lists and prices for cars and bikes. The Engine steam wash add-on is drive-through only.

Paying for your service

Pay with UPI, using any UPI app or the QR code, or choose to pay cash after the service. With cash, nothing is due online and your slot is held as soon as you confirm.

Plans changed?

Use your booking ID and the mobile number you booked with to check or cancel online. Cancelling frees the slot for someone else.
