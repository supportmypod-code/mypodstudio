Which package should you choose?

  • Regular upkeep: start with Basic Wash, or Express Plus when the dashboard and door pads also need cleaning and dressing.
  • A fresher interior and a shine on the paint: Detail Boost adds a hybrid ceramic wet wax or a synthetic sealant finish.
  • A tired interior: Interior Gold is a deep clean of seats, carpets, ceiling and AC vents. See what is included.
  • Dull or swirled paint: Exterior Gold and Ultimate Revival Detail cover paint correction. Read how polishing works.
