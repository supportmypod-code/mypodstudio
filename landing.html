      Frequently asked questions

      Do you repair laptops, computers and printers?

      Yes, from our New Town base. We diagnose first and confirm scope and fees before any work begins. Site visits are agreed case by case.

      What is Tech Care Club?

      An annual membership from ₹499, offered by Techonn. It includes standard check-ups, discounted repair labour and service reminders for your computer, laptop and mobile.

      Do repair discounts cover parts?

      No. Discounts apply to labour only. Parts and software licences are charged separately and quoted in advance. Deep cleaning, OS installation, data recovery and component replacement are paid services.

      How does print delivery work?

      Upload your files, select your New Town area, review the price and we deliver. ₹10 per page, six passport photos for ₹60, with a ₹99 minimum order. Delivery within a day, subject to demand.

      Where do you operate?

      Business compliance and online tech plans are delivered online across West Bengal. Repairs, printing and passport photos are available in Kolkata and New Town.

      Which business plan is right for me?

      Starter for new businesses. Professional if you also need Trade Licence, FSSAI or IEC. Business Plus for multiple entities or a dedicated manager.

      Are government fees included?

      No. Stamp duty, ROC and licence fees are charged at actuals and shared with you before work starts.

      Do you guarantee approval?

      No. Approvals are decided by the relevant departments. We file accurately and keep you informed at every stage.

      Is Udyam (MSME) registration really free?

      Yes. It is free with any service, for West Bengal businesses.

      Is Onnmore a government portal?

      No. Onnmore is an independent private company, not a government authority or portal.

      How do I track my case?

      Log in to the client portal with your email. Updates are also sent by email and WhatsApp.
